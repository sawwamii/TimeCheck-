Learn More on How to Use Our Calculator!

Read each step carefully below before trying it out:

Step 1: Input Your Hours

Enter the number of hours you spend on your phone or tablet each day. The calculator will automatically convert your input into daily hours!

Step 2: Compute and Check

Click the "Compute" button to calculate your total screen time. The calculator will display the total number of hours you spend on your device each day!

Step 3: Explore Your Options

After computing your screen time, the calculator will provide you with a list of fun and creative activities you can do with the calculated time!
