This website addresses a study on screen time done by the University of Otago!

They discovered that children in New Zealand spend a massive one-third of their after-school time on screens, doubling to over half their time after 8:00 pm. This excessive screen time takes a heavy toll on our sleep, mood, and physical health which is very alarming.
